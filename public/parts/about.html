Part data & credits

This software uses the LDraw Parts Library. The expanded part meshes and thumbnails are converted from LDraw geometry, with normalized positions and conservative collision cells.

Upright stud and socket connections are derived from the LDCad Shadow Library, by its contributors. Brickwork filters and transforms those connections to its stud-and-plate grid. Sideways connections, clips, hinges and sub-stud interlocking are not simulated.

Part names, known colors and LEGO element IDs use Rebrickable catalog data. Catalog colors and IDs do not guarantee current retailer availability. Displayed prices are planning estimates unless a seller quote has been recorded.
